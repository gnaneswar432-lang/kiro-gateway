RULES OF THE GAME

⚖️

Always Disclaim

"Educational only. Not financial advice. Not a SEBI registered advisor." On every post. Non-negotiable.

🎯

Don't Recommend Stocks

Teach concepts, not tips. SEBI banned stock recommendations from unregistered creators. Stay safe.

📊

Use Real Examples

Use real Indian companies (Reliance, TCS, Infosys) for examples — but for education, never as buy/sell calls.

🔁

Consistency > Perfection

An OK post posted today beats a perfect post posted next week. Ship daily.

💾

Build for Saves

Cheat sheets, glossaries, checklists get saved. Saves boost reach more than likes.

🪞

Stay Authentic

You're a learner, not a guru. Share that journey. People follow honesty in 2026.
