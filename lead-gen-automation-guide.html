1. What We're Building (The Big Picture)

Before we touch any tool, let's understand what we're creating and WHY it's valuable.

The Problem We're Solving

A business gets 50+ form submissions per day from their website. Manually reading each one, deciding who is a "hot lead" (ready to buy) vs "cold lead" (just browsing), and then following up takes 3+ hours every single day.

Our automation does all of this in seconds, 24/7, with zero human effort.

What the Automation Does (In Plain English)

1

Someone fills out a form on the business website

They enter their name, email, budget, what service they need, and a message.

2

AI reads their answers and scores them 1-10

Based on budget, urgency, and fit, AI decides: Is this person likely to buy? Score 8+? Hot lead. Score below 5? Cold lead.

3

Hot leads get an instant personalized email

Within 60 seconds of submitting the form, they get a friendly email: "Hi [name], thanks for reaching out! Based on what you shared, I'd love to schedule a quick call..."

4

Cold leads get a different nurture email

A softer email with helpful resources: "Hi [name], here's a free guide that might help you..."

5

All leads are saved to Google Sheets with their score

A clean spreadsheet with: Name, Email, Score, Category (Hot/Cold), AI's reasoning, date received.

Result: The business owner wakes up every morning to a spreadsheet of pre-scored, pre-contacted leads. They only spend time on the hot ones. This saves 20+ hours/week and increases sales by 30-50%.

2. What is Lead Generation Automation?

Breaking it down into simple words.

Lead = A potential customer

Anyone who shows interest in a business. They filled a form, sent a message, called, or signed up for something.

Lead Generation = Finding potential customers

The process of attracting people who might buy from you. Usually through ads, social media, websites, or referrals.

Lead Qualification = Scoring those customers

Deciding which leads are worth your time. A person with a $10,000 budget who needs help "this week" is hotter than someone "just looking around."

AI Automation = Making AI do all of this automatically

Instead of a human reading every form and deciding, AI does it instantly, 24/7, without breaks or mistakes.

5. Step-by-Step: Create the Lead Capture Form

This is what visitors fill out on the website. It collects the data AI will analyze.

Option A: Using Google Forms (Easiest, 100% Free)

1

Go to forms.google.com

Click the big "+" button to create a new blank form.

2

Set the title

Example: "Get a Free Consultation" or "Request a Quote"

3

Add these questions (in this exact order)

This order matters because AI will use all of this to score the lead.

#QuestionTypeWhy We Need It
1What's your full name?Short answerTo personalize emails
2What's your email address?Short answerTo send follow-up
3What's your company/business name?Short answerShows they're serious
4What service are you interested in?Multiple choice OR short answerTells AI what they want
5What's your approximate budget?Multiple choice: Under $500 / $500-$2,000 / $2,000-$5,000 / $5,000+ / Not sureKey scoring factor

7. Step-by-Step: Add AI to Qualify Leads

This is the brain of the automation. AI will read the form answers and score each lead.

1

Click the "+" next to the Google Forms module

This adds a new step AFTER the form trigger.

2

Search for "OpenAI" and select it

Choose the "OpenAI (ChatGPT, DALL-E, Whisper)" app.

3

Select "Create a Chat Completion"

This is the action that sends a message to ChatGPT and gets a response back.

4

Connect your OpenAI account

Click "Add" → paste your API key (the one you saved earlier: sk-xxxxx...) → click Save.

5

Select the model: "gpt-4o-mini"

This is the cheapest smart model. It costs about $0.001 per lead scored. Fast and accurate enough for lead scoring.

6

Write the System Prompt (THIS IS CRITICAL)

In the "System Message" field, paste the prompt below. This tells AI exactly how to score leads.

SYSTEM PROMPT — Copy this exactly
You are a lead qualification expert for a business. Your job is to read a potential customer's form submission and score them from 1 to 10 based on how likely they are to become a paying customer. SCORING RULES: - Budget $5,000+ AND needs help this week/month = Score 9-10 - Budget $2,000-$5,000 AND needs help this month = Score 7-8 - Budget $500-$2,000 AND needs help in 2-3 months = Score 5-6 - Budget under $500 OR "just exploring" = Score 3-4 - Vague answers, no budget, unclear needs = Score 1-2 You MUST respond in EXACTLY this format (no extra text): SCORE: [number] CATEGORY: [Hot/Warm/Cold] REASON: [one sentence explaining why] SUGGESTED_REPLY: [a personalized 2-sentence response to send them] Categories: - Score 7-10 = Hot - Score 4-6 = Warm - Score 1-3 = Cold
7

Write the User Message (Form data goes here)

In the "User Message" field, you'll insert the actual form data. Click in the field and use Make.com's data picker to insert variables:

USER MESSAGE — Use Make.com variables (the purple tags)
New lead submission: Name: {{Name}} Email: {{Email}} Company: {{Company}} Service Interested In: {{Service}} Budget: {{Budget}} Timeline: {{How soon do you need this?}} Additional Details: {{Tell us more about what you need}} Please score this lead.
How to insert variables: When you click inside the User Message field in Make.com, you'll see a panel showing data from the previous module (Google Forms). Click each piece of data to insert it. They appear as colored tags like Name.
8

Click OK to save the module

You now have: Form Trigger → AI Scoring. Two modules connected by a line.

What happens now: When someone submits the form, AI receives all their answers, thinks about it, and outputs something like:

SCORE: 8
CATEGORY: Hot
REASON: High budget ($5,000+) with urgent timeline (this week). Clear project description shows readiness to buy.
SUGGESTED_REPLY: Hi Sarah, thanks for reaching out! Your project sounds exciting and we'd love to help. Let me schedule a quick 15-minute call this week to discuss the details.

8. Step-by-Step: Route Leads (Hot vs Cold)

Now we need to split the path: hot leads go one way, cold leads go another.

Understanding the Router

A "Router" in Make.com splits your automation into multiple paths based on conditions. Think of it like a traffic light: green light = go this way, red light = go that way.

1

Right-click the line between OpenAI module and the empty space

Or: Click the "+" after the OpenAI module, then search for "Router" in the Tools section.

2

Add a Router module

The Router appears as a diamond shape with two (or more) output paths. You'll see two lines coming out of it.

3

Set up Path 1: HOT LEADS (score 7+)

Click on the first path line. A filter settings panel opens. Set it up as:

Label: Hot Lead

Condition: The OpenAI response → "contains" → "CATEGORY: Hot"

4

Set up Path 2: COLD/WARM LEADS

Click on the second path line. Set the filter:

Label: Cold/Warm Lead

Condition: The OpenAI response → "does not contain" → "CATEGORY: Hot"

What this does: Every time AI scores a lead, the router checks: Did AI say "CATEGORY: Hot"? If yes → go to Hot path. If no → go to Cold path. Simple!
What Your Canvas Should Look Like Now: [Google Forms] → [OpenAI] → [Router Diamond] → Two paths going in different directions

13. How to Improve It Over Time

Once it's running, here's how to make it even better.

Level 1: Quick Improvements (Do This Week)

Add a Slack/Telegram notification for hot leads

Get instantly notified on your phone when a hot lead comes in. Add a Slack or Telegram module on the hot path: "New HOT lead: {{Name}} (Score: 9). Check your email!"

Improve your AI prompt based on real results

After 20-30 leads, review the scores. Is AI scoring too high? Too low? Adjust the scoring rules in your system prompt.

Add a "Warm" path

Instead of just Hot and Cold, add a third path for Warm leads (score 4-6) with a medium-urgency email.

Level 2: Advanced Improvements (Next Month)

Add a CRM integration

Instead of (or in addition to) Google Sheets, send leads directly to a CRM like HubSpot, Pipedrive, or Salesforce.

Add a follow-up sequence

If a hot lead doesn't reply in 48 hours, automatically send a follow-up email. Add a "Delay" module (wait 48 hours) then another Gmail module.

Add a booking link with Calendly

Instead of "reply to this email," include a Calendly link so hot leads can book a call instantly. Track who books.

Score based on website behavior

Advanced: Track which pages they visited before filling the form. Someone who viewed your pricing page 3 times = extra hot.

15. Troubleshooting Common Problems

Things that might go wrong and exactly how to fix them.

Problem: "My automation didn't trigger"

Cause: The scheduling is off, or Google Forms connection broke.

Fix: Check that scheduling toggle is ON (blue). Check the Google Forms connection — you may need to reconnect it. Click on the module and verify the connection shows "Connected."

Problem: "AI gives weird or inconsistent scores"

Cause: Your prompt is too vague or the AI model is too weak.

Fix: Make your scoring rules MORE specific in the prompt. Add examples: "Someone who says '$5,000+ budget' and 'this week' should ALWAYS be score 8+." Also try switching from gpt-4o-mini to gpt-4o (costs more but smarter).

Problem: "The router sends everything to one path"

Cause: Your filter condition doesn't match the AI's response format.

Fix: Click on the OpenAI module's output after a test run. Look at the EXACT text AI returns. Then make your router filter match EXACTLY that text. Common issue: extra spaces or line breaks.

Problem: "Emails going to spam"

Cause: Gmail sending to strangers often lands in spam initially.

Fix: 1) Don't use ALL CAPS in subject lines. 2) Avoid spam words ("FREE", "BUY NOW"). 3) Send from a proper business email (hello@yourbusiness.com vs random123@gmail.com). 4) Over time, as people reply, deliverability improves.

Problem: "Make.com shows an error on OpenAI module"

Cause: API key expired, rate limit hit, or account has no credits.

Fix: Go to platform.openai.com → Billing. Make sure you have credits loaded ($5 minimum). If your key expired, create a new one and update it in Make.com.

Problem: "I ran out of Make.com free operations"

Cause: Free plan has 1,000 operations/month. Each run uses ~5 operations.

Fix: Upgrade to the $9/month plan (10,000 operations) when you're ready. By the time you hit this limit, you should already have a paying client covering this cost.

Problem: "Google Sheets not updating"

Cause: Wrong spreadsheet selected, or column headers don't match.

Fix: Open the Google Sheets module in Make.com. Verify: correct spreadsheet selected, correct sheet tab (Sheet1), and that your column headers in Row 1 match EXACTLY what you set up.

Golden Rule: When something breaks, click on the failed module (it shows red). Read the error message. 90% of the time, the error tells you exactly what's wrong.
